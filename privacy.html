Privacy policy: lenovo mailq

lenovo mailq is a private, single-user tool. Its only user is the owner of the Gmail mailbox it is connected to.

What it accesses

With the owner's permission (Google scope gmail.modify), it reads, labels and archives messages in that one mailbox, and sends replies from it. It cannot permanently delete mail or change account settings.

Where data goes

Messages are processed on the owner's own server. The data is not sold, shared or used for advertising. The data is also not used to train general AI models. Use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

The owner can revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
